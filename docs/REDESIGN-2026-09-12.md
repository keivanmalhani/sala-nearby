# The 12 September redesign

Ten things he asked for on 12 September, in his order, and what each one becomes. Written
before any code so the direction is fixed in one place and later passes do not drift.

## The look, committed to

A cinema lobby with the house lights down. The dark theme he likes stays the reference:
warm near-black ground, ivory type, and the amber "lamp" as the marquee bulb that marks
what is on or selected. What changes is that colour starts to carry meaning instead of one
amber doing every job:

- **Amber (lamp)** is selection and action: the lit chip, the selected tab, the primary
  button, and the one number on a row you act on, the count.
- **Cool projector blue (beam)** is place and distance: where distances are measured from,
  walking times, the location control, the map pin for the origin.
- **Features are filled, facts are outlined**, list and sheet alike: IMAX in beam blue,
  Atmos in violet, Platino in ember and Subtitled in green are solid; Premium, Standard, 3D
  and Dubbed are quiet outlines, because they describe the room rather than change the
  decision, and dubbed is the default in this city.
- Light mode stops being beige on beige: white surfaces on a warm ground, stronger ink, and
  badges with enough contrast to read in daylight.

Type does not change. Bricolage Grotesque, Newsreader and IBM Plex Mono already give the
app its voice and none of them is a default.

## The ten asks

1. **Swipe down to refresh.** Pull on Movies or Showtimes: it asks the site for a newer
   build, reloads if there is one, and otherwise says when the showtimes were read. That
   is only worth having if the listings actually get newer on their own, so the refresh
   scripts move to a daily scheduled run on GitHub's machines and publish without the
   laptop. Until that run exists the pull is honest about the date rather than pretending.
2. **Better colouring.** The system above.
3. **Tabs, left to right: Movies, Showtimes, Map, Settings.** "Films" is renamed Movies
   because that is his word, and the app opens on Movies, the leftmost tab.
4. **More useful info.** When a showing ends, from the billed time and the running time;
   what a Mexican age rating means (A, AA, B, B15, C, D); the IMAX specifics in 10.
5. **Distances from, typed.** Tapping "Distances from" opens a place picker: type a
   neighbourhood, a Metro station or a landmark and pick it from suggestions that come from
   a gazetteer built into the app, so it works with no signal. Each suggestion says how
   many cinemas are a walk from there, which is the question the picker exists to answer.
   Current location and Parque Mexico stay one tap away, and a picked place can be saved
   under a name he chooses. Saved places live only on the phone.
6. **The theme toggle leaves the header.**
7. **A Settings tab**, holding appearance (system, light, dark), language, where distances
   are measured from, and when the showtimes were read.
8. **A better search bar.** Suggestions while typing, grouped into movies, directors,
   cinemas and genres; a clear button; a cinema suggestion opens that cinema.
9. **English and Spanish.** Every control, label and count line in both, chosen in
   Settings and defaulting to the phone's language. The long audit notes are translated in
   a later pass, and until then say so rather than mixing languages mid-sentence.
10. **Specific about IMAX.** Per IMAX room: laser 4K or xenon 2K, screen size, sound,
    each with where the fact came from and how sure it is. Unknown stays unknown.

## Order of work

Structure first because everything else hangs off it, then the rest in roughly the order
it changes his use of the app: tabs and Settings; colour; the place picker; search; pull to
refresh and the daily run; Spanish; IMAX and the extra info. Each lands as its own patch
script and paired test, like every earlier change, and is checked at phone width in both
themes before it ships.
